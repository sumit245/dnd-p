# Trustpilot company description

For the **Company description** field ("About Dashandots — written by the
company"). Trustpilot recommends 200+ words; this is 268. Everything in it is
checkable against the site.

---

## Plain text version (paste this if HTML is not enabled on your plan)

Dashandots Technology Private Limited is a custom software development company
in Gurugram, Delhi NCR. We build the systems that small and mid-sized Indian
businesses run their day on.

Most of our clients come to us at the same point: the business has outgrown
spreadsheets and a collection of disconnected tools, but packaged software
would mean changing how they work to suit the product. We build the system
around the business instead.

What we develop: custom ERP and CRM platforms; hospital management systems
covering OPD, IPD, pharmacy, lab and billing; manufacturing ERP with bill of
materials, job cards and production planning; transport management systems for
fleet operators; hotel property management; e-commerce portals; WhatsApp
Business API platforms; dashboards and reporting; Android and web applications;
and IoT and embedded work.

Two things we do differently, and you can check both before spending anything.
First, we have working demos of our systems online rather than slide decks, so
you can click through a hospital or transport system and decide whether the
workflow matches yours. Second, we put ownership in writing: the source code
and the intellectual property are yours, the repository sits in your account,
and we hand over build instructions and deployment access whether or not you
keep us on for support.

We work with manufacturers, hospitals, logistics operators, recyclers, training
providers and membership organisations across India. If a process in your
business still runs on WhatsApp and Excel, that is usually the right place to
start.

## On the reviews themselves

I have not written review text, and I would not: a review has to come from the
person who used the service, in their words. Trustpilot detects and removes
company-written reviews, and a profile caught doing it carries a public warning
banner — worse than having no reviews at all.

What works instead is asking, immediately after something goes right. Use
Trustpilot's own invitation system so the reviews carry the "Verified" tag.

**Invitation message to send clients:**

> Hi [name],
>
> Now that [the HMS rollout / the app launch / phase one] is live and your team
> is using it, would you mind leaving us a review on Trustpilot? It takes a
> couple of minutes and it genuinely helps people who are deciding whether to
> build custom software at all.
>
> [Trustpilot invitation link]
>
> Please write it as you found it — the useful ones say what the problem was
> and what changed, not that everything was perfect.
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]

Send it to the people you already know are happy: Sugs Lloyd, Trust Me Recycle,
SP Constructs, Skill Bridge India, Snüb Foods. Five honest reviews mentioning
ERP, a hospital system or a field app will do more for a buyer's confidence
than fifty generic five-star lines, and far more than anything written in-house.
